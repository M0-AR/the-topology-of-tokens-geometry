Model: ordinary least squares on log(bytes/token) versus log(vocab) using six tokenizers per corpus. Measured points: 8,192, 16,384 and 32,768. 32k extrapolation from 8k/16k had MAPE 2.3% (BPE) and 1.9% (Unigram). A pooled cross-corpus model did not generalize (LOCO R² < 0), so this calculator is corpus-specific by design.
